Why it exists

I built TEOLANDS for my son. He loved world-map conquest games, but the fast ones were mostly about who clicks quickest, and another map strategy game was too complicated and not much to look at. So I made something in between: calmer, with real decisions, and a map we both enjoy looking at.

A dad in Latvia.

Questions

Before you install

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no ads and no in-game purchases.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game, the tutorial and its narration all run offline. LAN games for 2 to 4 players use your home network only.

Why does my computer show a warning?

These early builds are not yet signed, so Windows and macOS do not recognise the publisher. Each release lists SHA-256 checksums so you can check a download. Windows: choose More info → Run anyway. Mac: try opening TEOLANDS once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Which Macs are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) on macOS 13 or later.

How do I play with friends?

Two to four computers with the same TEOLANDS version on the same home network. One player hosts from the main menu and the others join. The host keeps the shared save.

Is there a browser version?

Not yet. It is planned; for now TEOLANDS runs on Windows and Apple Silicon Macs.

What are scenario packs?

Add-on historical campaigns for the desktop game, with their own starting borders and goals. They are coming soon.

Will my saves survive an update?

Yes. Install the new version over the old one; your saves stay in your user folder.
